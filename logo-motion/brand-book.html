01 · The mark

A keycap with one job

The mark is the Shift key — a soft ocean keycap carrying an ink arrow that always points up. Three shapes, nothing else: the base, the cap, the arrow. It reads at any size and never appears without all three (except in single-color reproduction).

Cap — ocean Arrow Base

Anatomy

Base #2563EB · Cap #3B82F6 · Arrow #FFFFFF. The base peeks out below-right, giving the key its press.

Clearspace

Keep a margin of half the cap's width clear on every side. No text, marks, or motion debris inside it.

hero ≥ 96px
ui 44px
min 24px / 10mm

Minimum sizes

Never smaller than 24px digital or 10mm print. Below that, drop the base and keep cap + arrow.

03 · Color

11 · Slogan

Shift happens.

Two words and a period. It's a shrug and a promise at once — change is constant, and we're the key you press to meet it.

Shift happens.
Shift Labs

Always

  • Sentence case, always with the period: "Shift happens."
  • "happens." may take Ocean when set on dark.
  • Pair with the mark or the wordmark — never both.
  • Let it stand alone. It is a complete sentence.

Never

  • No exclamation mark. It's a fact, not a pitch.
  • Don't extend it: "Shift really happens!" — no.
  • Don't title-case or uppercase it in running copy.
  • Don't use it as a CTA button label.
12 · Voice

How we sound

Direct, warm, a little wry. We state facts and let them land — the slogan is the compression of the whole voice: two words, one period.

We are

  • Direct — say the thing, then stop.
  • Warm — a person, not a platform.
  • Wry — one raised eyebrow, never a wink-wink.
  • Concrete — keys, presses, ships. No synergy.

We are not

  • Loud — no exclamation marks, ever (see Slogan).
  • Corporate — "leverage" is a lever, not a verb.
  • Cute-for-cute's-sake — the pun must carry meaning.
  • Vague — if it can't be pressed, don't promise it.

13 · Emoji

The reaction set

Emoji are voice, compressed further. Same rules apply: direct, warm, wry — and quiet. One emoji per message, at the end, never in headlines. Every pick below maps to something the brand already owns.

⬆️

The signature — ⬆️

The up arrow is the mark itself, so it's our default reaction: it means shipped, agreed, upvoted, onwards. When in doubt, react with ⬆️ and say nothing. In running text, prefer the real key glyph ⇧ (U+21E7) — it's a character, not an emoji: "press ⇧ Enter", "⇧ v2.1 is live."

In chat

Reactions do the celebrating so the message doesn't have to. ⬆️ is the team's +1.

Always

  • One emoji per message, and it goes last.
  • React with ⬆️ instead of replying "nice".
  • 🧪 marks anything beta, flagged, or experimental.
  • ⇧ (the character) in docs and UI copy; ⬆️ (the emoji) in chat and social.

Never

  • No emoji in headlines, error messages, or legal copy.
  • No stacking: "shipped ⬆️🎉🔥💯" — that's shouting.
  • Don't replace words with emoji mid-sentence.
  • Don't use ⬇️ about anything. Ever.

06 · Closing

Key, slogan, one contact line. The slogan lives here or on the title — never both.

Always

  • One idea per slide; the headline is the takeaway.
  • Pick one colorway per deck and stay in it.
  • Body text ≥ 24pt in the real deck — if it doesn't fit, cut words.
  • Charts in Ocean on the theme background, one series highlighted.

Never

  • No slogan on every slide — it's a closer, not a watermark.
  • No drop shadows, gradients on text, or clip art.
  • Don't mix Midnight and Paper slides in one deck.
  • Don't shrink the key below 24px to squeeze in content.
